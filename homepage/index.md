## Work

### CARE

Competence-aware reward shaping for adaptive reasoning length in Video-MLLMs. Designed dynamic rewards for GRPO-based reasoning control, improved reasoning efficiency and answer accuracy in video QA, and validated behavior through ablations and case analyses.

[View code](https://github.com/1Pansy/Video-CARE)

### VideoDR Benchmark

Open-web video deep research benchmark for agentic video reasoning. Co-developed benchmark tasks, built agents combining video understanding, web retrieval, and multi-hop reasoning, and designed evaluation for retrieval consistency and evidence grounding.

[View code](https://github.com/QuantaAlpha/VideoDR-Benchmark)

### Graph-Augmented Product Bundling

LLM and graph architecture for product bundle construction. Combined Qwen2, LightGCN, and text-form graph injection; integrated MCTS strategies during decoding; evaluated on Alibaba POG and Steam datasets.

### Dialogue Generation

Iterative prompt-enhanced large model dialogue generation. Built vLLM-based inference pipelines, used multi-sample generation and draft optimization, and evaluated on DailyDialog with Alpaca-7B and Vicuna-13B.
